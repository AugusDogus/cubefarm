# Network constraints: adversarial implementation review

Scope: coordination, work-funded replication, routing opportunity costs, and earned delegation. Production files were read only. Measurements use actual `act`/`advance` integration or the exported network simulator. The submitted save was read and parsed without alteration.

## Design standard

The [Paperclips audit](18-paperclips-source-audit.md) identifies two relevant principles: resource coupling must keep expansion consequential after automation, and delegation should follow understanding of the task. A slower timer does not establish either. Choices should win under different ordinary conditions, with costs visible before commitment and recovery available without a repetitive purchasing chore.

The historical source directory referenced in that audit was absent after the server restart during this review. This review does not claim to have independently re-read those bundles. Its direct evidence is the current Cube Farm implementation and reproducible simulations.

Final implementation disposition: the source archive has since been restored and verified. Growth, automatic investment, and intensive routes reserve maximum future coordination load. Standard Clear with manual investment can reserve the current load because its load decreases. Routing sacrifices scale with funded bonus work; an unfunded route falls back to ordinary production. Historical fixture credit is explicitly 1,000. New delegation requires 10,000 completed workflows while migrated standing settings remain honored. Conditional routing tests retain the unchanged completion, cadence, action, and maintenance ceilings. Current measurements are in `BALANCE.md`; the intermediate numbers below remain historical evidence.

## Findings and required dispositions

1. **Preset stability promises must cover expansion.** `networkPlan` originally used the current stability requirement plus two percentage points. Coordination can subsequently increase that requirement by twelve points, or six with Institutional memory. In a legal domain state with 5,000 offices, all procedures, ample resources, and Grow, 300 seconds of Standard operation reached full load and lost approximately 1,751 offices. Clear stayed at zero load and lost none. Use a bounded future-load margin for presets, and retain manual allocations as the deliberate risk. Do not solve this by hiding losses or automatically changing a player's allocation.
2. **Historical fixture credit must match migration.** The compact reported fixture inherited the fresh buffer of 40 credits from `initialNetwork`, but the actual attachment has no credit field and migrates to 1,000. Assign the historical credit explicitly or compare the parsed attachment. Otherwise a claimed reported-run comparison changes the starting constraints.
3. **Unfunded routing still has an opportunity cost.** Survey cuts clearance capacity to 65%; Parallel cuts discovery capacity to 65%, even when compute or knowledge reserve prevents extra work. “Fall back to normal work” is therefore misleading. Explain that the selected diversion remains until the player changes routing, or implement a genuinely conditional diversion. The former is the smaller, clearer choice.
4. **A routing choice must not be required to always win.** The original pacing regression assumed Survey then Parallel always completed faster. With meaningful diversion, that assertion tests a dominance the design is meant to remove. Replace it with explicit conditional rate and research/clearance tradeoffs. Preserve the existing four-hour completion ceiling, action/switch budgets, zero-maintenance requirement, finite-pool conservation, and starting-state immutability.
5. **Earned delegation must tolerate existing settings.** New games should experience the work-funded constraint before automatic building/investment unlocks at 10,000 cleared workflows. Existing `balanced` or `research-first` preferences must continue to operate. Do not reject historical saves below the new threshold or silently turn preferences off.

## Measured conditional choices

Before the test controller was updated from a fixed route recipe, a legal extraction entry gave these results:

| Policy | Completion | Next procedure |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Manual, Standard | 1,154s | 307s |
| Research first, Standard | 1,109s | 307s |
| Research first, Survey then Parallel | 1,242s | 274s |

Survey accelerated research while delaying completion. This is a real opportunity cost, rather than an unconditional upgrade. A route focused on research can be useful before procedures are deployed without being the fastest clearance recipe.

The actual submitted save, parsed with the historical credit migration, produced the following intermediate implementation results after the controller switched to sixty-second routing forecasts:

| Policy | Completion | Next procedure | Commissioned | Commission cash |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Manual, Standard | 990s | 216s | 0 | $0 |
| Research first, Standard | 644s | 216s | 12,080 | $9,421,645,000 |
| Research first, Adaptive | 652s | 216s | 12,152 | $9,532,484,500 |

These are intermediate measurements, not final balance values. Each policy started with the same parsed save; serializing the starting state after the runs confirmed no mutation. High coordination persisted at the end of both funded runs. Their lack of new losses depended on the controller's allocation changes, not on the original Grow preset being safe at all future sizes.

## Tests must establish mechanics, not enjoyment

Useful independent checks include: growth-only allocation eventually exhausts its fresh credit; processing replenishes it without a purchase; a preset at maximum load does not lose offices at fixed pressure; both intense routes sacrifice another productive department with abundant resources; reserves prevent overspending while retaining the stated sacrifice; migrated settings remain operational; and all legal policies finish within unchanged cadence budgets.

This design adds recoverable consequences and competing work priorities. Mechanical completion, source similarities, and adversarial review do not establish whether people find it compelling. Avoid presenting reduced completion time alone as evidence of fun.

## Final pacing disposition

The full integrated suite passes the original cadence limits after the final procedure's explicit 20-to-24-times productivity adjustment at its unchanged knowledge price. Six fresh runs earn their first additional procedure within 300 seconds and retain a maximum mechanical gap of 570 seconds. A prescribed Grow refresh after Institutional memory was rejected because lowering stabilization slows coordination repair. The visible optional comparison now discloses that tradeoff. The controller does not force it. Current exact-save and conditional-routing measurements are in BALANCE.md; previous tables above are intermediate evidence.
